Our story

How it started

DKF was born from a simple conviction: wealth should be a vehicle for healing. Founded by three women with roots in design, business and healthcare, DKF brings together people who want to make a difference and the institutions and ventures doing the work.

We don't collect or manage funds. We open doors — introducing donors to hospitals such as Sheba, connecting investors with Health-Tech companies, and raising awareness of where support matters most.

Every connection we make flows back to one purpose: better healthcare for more people.

“Philanthropy is not about money… it's about using whatever resources you have at your fingertips and applying them to improving the world.” — Melinda French Gates
